Why only you can confirm

Your confirmation is a self statement: something you say about yourself. The Tide network will only sign it if the person signing is you. Your provider, the agency and system admins can't confirm on your behalf.

If you have a plan nominee, they sign in with their own account and confirm as your nominee.
