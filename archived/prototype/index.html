How a claim gets paid

Worker signs

"I delivered this support."

Participant confirms

"Yes, this happened." Only the participant can sign this.

Provider submits

"This matches the service agreement."

Agency approves

"This passes the funding rules."

Why you can trust it

Each step is signed through the Tide network, not by our server or any one organisation. If anyone changes a signed claim afterwards, even a system administrator, the signatures stop matching and payment is blocked.
